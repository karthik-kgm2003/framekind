Are these official platform safe zones?+

No. Every preset is an editable, illustrative starting point. The example interface is a simplified mockup, not a current screenshot or an exact replica. The values have not been measured or certified by Instagram, TikTok, or YouTube.

Device, app version, caption length, language, accessibility settings, ads, and account features can change what overlaps your creative. Confirm inside the real app. Only the 9:16 canvas shows platform mockups; other ratios are generic crop previews.

For paid ads, consult TikTok's ad guidance ↗ and Google's vertical-video guidance ↗. Ad guidance does not establish universal organic-post margins.

Do my photos or videos leave my device?+

No media is uploaded by this tool. Files are read into browser memory, and exports are generated on your device. This version has no analytics, advertising scripts, tracking cookies, or remote fonts. Your hosting provider may still record ordinary website access logs.

Using an external support or partner link opens a separate provider with its own privacy policy. Read our for details.

Can I export a video?+

Not in this version. You can load a browser-supported MP4, WebM, or MOV file, move the timeline to a frame, and export that frame as PNG. Codec support depends on your browser; MOV and some phone videos may need conversion first.

Video playback, audio editing, and full-video rendering are not included. PNG guide exports can be layered in your own video editor, then removed before publishing.

What's included in each download?+

Layout preview: your creative plus the currently enabled guides, mock UI, grid, and test headline. A reference label is included.

Clean image: only your crop and canvas background, without any test text or overlays. Transparent guide: a shaded guide with a transparent center and no creative. Comparison sheet: all three vertical platform layouts, with the same crop settings.

Larger exports do not restore detail missing from a small original. Previews and guides are planning references, not publishing guarantees.

Is it really free? What's the catch?+

The core tool and all PNG exports are free. No signup, subscription, or payment is required. Framekind is an independent project. Optional support helps its developer keep working on it, but it never unlocks basic functionality.
